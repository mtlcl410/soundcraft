## What this PR does

Follows external MIDI Beat Clock for playback and audio/MIDI recording. Start, Continue, Stop and Song Position Pointer control transport on a live beat grid. The project retains its saved tempo. Coast keeps a take open through clock loss; Stop on loss commits it. MTC Online follows an absolute position, so a new locate can displace an open take and recording requires going offline. Part of #1661. Fixes #1674. Fixes #1675.

## Hardware status

A TASCAM Model 12 sent standard Clock through Roland UM-ONE. The 8.000 s restart produced the S-MU2000 crash once; the 8.125 s restart skipped it. At 120 and 100 BPM the MAIN capture contained all eight snares. Clock-follow recording was exercised with simulated input. The final jitter, ramp and output-buffer matrices used rendered PCM and synthetic driver timing. Akai MPC hardware remains untested.

## Known limits

Grouped 180→60 BPM Clock delivery produced an attack 196.896 ms early. With ±1/±2 ms input jitter, the later peaks were 4.750/4.188 ms. Once audio reaches the device queue its timing is fixed. In the 20% slowdown matrix the first offbeat spanned −25.000…+86.500 ms, and the next beat −25.000…+83.771 ms. The late extremes occurred after returning to the one-pulse limit (300 BPM, 2,048 frames, 50 ms lead). One of twelve 256-frame takes had an unexplained waveform deviation; the 40 near-silent samples in the 64-frame run were a separate event (#1686). Ordinary tempo-map editing on main is tracked independently in #785 and #1690.

## Open questions

- Should a running Coast transport relock to returning F8 without another Start or Continue?
- Should tempo-follow response be fixed or adjustable? The ±8% ramp peak is 3.8333 ms; Bitwig offers a [synchronization response slider](https://www.bitwig.com/userguide/latest/the_dashboard/).
- Is prediction to the measured deadline acceptable for stable Clock with a large buffer, given the early and late limits above? A buffer shorter than one pulse always retains the hard cap.
- Should local looping stay inactive under external positioning? Should Song Position Pointer during playback continue to be ignored?
- Should Stop send individual Note Offs in addition to resetting the plug-in?
- How much tempo history should long sets retain?
- Should invalid follow preferences be recovered individually?
