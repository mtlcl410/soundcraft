## What this PR does

Generate MIDI Time Code (Gen MTC) or follow a forward source for playback (Online). Choose the device in Setup › Peripherals › Synchronization. Session Setup holds the frame rate, start, signed external offset and Freewheel policy. Stopped cursor moves send full-frame locates. Rolling output sends quarter frames. The six rates are 23.976, 24, 25, 29.97 non-drop, 29.97 drop-frame and 30 fps. MTC and Beat Clock share a connection and per-port calibration. Chase runs at the audio sample rate after locating. Online recording is disabled because a subsequent relocation can move the recording position.

## Hardware status

Earlier Windows runs used a TASCAM Model 12 and Roland UM-ONE. The physical loop exercised all six output settings, drop-frame minute and tenth-minute locates, and shared-port calibration. With the recorder sending 30 fps, chase locked and recovered through cable removal in None, Frames (8) and Jam Sync. Three USB STORAGE recoveries had a 0.140 ms between-take spread at the digital playback tap. Ten SD recordings had a 42.875 ms onset spread and missed the repeatability target. The recovery from a 100 ms output timing gap was tested in software.

## Known limits

Forward playback chase can relocate audibly under sustained drift. Shared sample clock may be needed for tight continuous sync. Fractional non-drop rates share MIDI wire codes with their integer partners. External LTC, MMC and MTC Online recording are outside this PR. Frames (8) briefly lost lock again on one reconnection. The cause is unresolved.
