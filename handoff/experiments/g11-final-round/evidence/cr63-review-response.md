## Reviewer reply draft

The update addresses transport, callback and device recovery during MIDI synchronization. Recorded channel messages take their time under the take-writer lock. Forward MTC keeps its absolute reader day through a short dropout over midnight. The output scheduler checks that its tempo map and transport pass match audible audio, and retries a failed Stop before resuming a new pass.

At 120 BPM in the compensated Clock tests, a 100 ms or 200 ms timestamp interruption still delivers all 49 expected F8 pulses over the one-second observation. The interval during repayment is 17–18 ms for the 100 ms gap and 15 ms for the 200 ms gap, after which spacing returns to the source grid. A separate test removes a 100 ms timestamp gap, lets repayment begin, and then prevents the worker from running from 240 ms to 1,200 ms. The repayment beat has already expired when the worker next gets CPU time. It sends Stop, SPP and Continue at the current position rather than replaying old debt.

On 81613837, the complete playback suite passed 219 tests with two OS-dependent cases ignored, and the UI suite passed all 390. The application suite passed all 58 tests. Formatting and Clippy for the modified playback and UI crates passed. The engine suite passed the conflicting-offset regression and 450 other tests. Its existing movie-relink fixture failed separately. This review used software regressions; the hardware measurements in the original PR were not rerun against this revision.
