## What this PR does

Follow an external MIDI Beat Clock for playback and recording. EXT shows Waiting, Locked or Coasting. Coast keeps an open take running and relocks on F8 alone; Stop on loss finishes the take. External position overrides the local loop, rolling SPP is ignored, and Stop releases held notes before resetting instruments. The tempo response is fixed. Invalid follow settings recover field by field with a visible notice. Fixes #1674 and #1675.

Audio and MIDI can record because the beat grid follows the incoming Clock. In MTC Online (#1663), an absolute locate could displace an open take. Pro Tools documents Clock transmission on pp. 743–744; the MPC guide disables audio recording while receiving Clock. The audio/MIDI tests are `coast_keeps_the_audio_and_midi_take_through_clock_loss_and_continue` and `stop_on_clock_loss_commits_the_audio_and_midi_take`. [Pro Tools 2026.4](https://resources.avid.com/SupportFiles/PT/Pro_Tools_Reference_Guide_2026.4.pdf) · [MPC v3.5, p. 60](https://cdn.inmusicbrands.com/Software/SME0-3.5.0/Public/MPC%20Standalone%20OS%20-%20User%20Guide%20-%20v3.5.pdf#page=60)

## Known limits

In 16 batched slowdowns the largest observed delivery delay was 166.667 ms; after subtracting it the largest absolute error was 0.000 ms. A locked, steady source predicts only to the device deadline. Over 18 device configurations the worst predictive output after slowing was +86.083 ms at the first offbeat and +83.646 ms at the first beat. For live use, try 64–256 frames where supported. #1686 tracks one unexplained difference in twelve 256-frame takes. A separate 64-frame capture had 40 near-silent samples.
